      How the numbers are made

      Every completed Squeek block issues SQK, shared by power: share = W / (W + everyone else), with W = P + S × (1 + P / P_ref) / c. P is the NFT power of your deposited Squeeks, S your staked SQK, P_ref = 1,000.

      • The formula was checked against Staking.quotePower on the Sepolia testnet, and reproduces the whitepaper's own concentration example (96.5%).
      • Level power 1,000 to 1,207,269 (×2.2 a level), merge cooldowns and the three-year halving schedule come from the whitepaper; the testnet uses the same values.
      • One account deposits at most 100 Squeeks (Staking.MAX_TOKENS on the testnet).
      • Not final: staking is "in active development". c, the exit fee, the launch bonus and the year length are TBD or proposed, and an older docs page still describes 6,900 SQK per block with no staking.
      • Not modelled: the exit fee on unstaking, the stakers' share of the pool's swap fees, gas, and prices.
